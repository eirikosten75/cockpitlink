# Code of Conduct

Virpil LED Bridge is a small hobby project for flight-sim enthusiasts. Everyone is welcome here – whatever your
experience, background or which aircraft you fly.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project – issues, pull requests, discussions – to be friendly, helpful and free of
harassment for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Many users are new to Python, GitHub or SimConnect.
- Assume good intent, and give and take constructive feedback gracefully.
- Stay on topic and keep discussions about the project.
- Respect that the maintainer works on this in their spare time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, harassment, trolling or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, or anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code of conduct, and may
block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code of conduct, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment (the `…` menu), or
[report the user to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
You can also open an issue asking the maintainer to get in touch – please leave out the details in a public issue.
Reports are handled as discreetly as possible.
