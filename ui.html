Aircraft profiles

A profile has extra rules for some aircraft – for example a different parking brake light in the A320. Its rules take over the LEDs they drive; every other LED keeps the rules for all aircraft. Off: the rules for all aircraft are used for every aircraft, as before. Your profiles are kept when you turn this off.

Automatic switching picks the profile from the aircraft name, so it needs MSFS running and the bridge connected through SimConnect. You can also choose a profile by hand at the top of the page or in the tray menu; that choice stays until you choose Automatic again.

Make and edit profiles in the Rules tab.

Back up and share

Save your whole setup in one file – devices, LED names, rules, aircraft profiles, base colours, your own colours and drawings – to keep a copy, move it to another PC or share it. To share one panel, use Export on its card in the Panels tab.

Importing shows what is in the file first. You choose per device whether it replaces the matching device or is skipped; config.json and layouts.json are backed up before anything is changed.
